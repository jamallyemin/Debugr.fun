          PRIVACY

          On your device only

          Your progress, settings, saved playground code and lesson answers live in this browser (local storage). They go nowhere unless you link Google (see below).

          Stored online (Firebase, run by Google)

          • an anonymous player id, your nickname and profile picture
          • scores, ratings, streaks and the daily challenge results
          • rooms you join: your nickname, progress and quick messages while you race
          • friend codes, friend requests and your friend list
          • a one-word activity note ("in a room") and a time, readable only by your friends. Turn it off in Settings.
          • feedback you send, plus technical details if you leave that box ticked

          If you link Google

          Firebase keeps your Google account email to sign you in, and a cloud save of your progress (puzzles, stats, lessons finished, picture, nickname).

          Other services the page contacts

          Firebase and Google (the game's backend), Google Fonts (the typefaces), jsDelivr (the Python runtime for Learn). They see your IP address like any website you visit. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking cookies.

          Deleting your data

          Settings can reset what is on this device. To remove your online data (nickname, scores, friends, cloud save), send feedback with your nickname or open an issue on GitHub and it will be deleted.

          ! HEADS UP

          This site uses keyboard shortcuts: single keys like S, M, D and 1–6, and in games Ctrl+S, Ctrl+Enter and Alt+H.

          They can clash with your browser, your extensions or a screen reader. For example Ctrl+S normally saves the page, but in a game we use it to shove the bug back.

          While a game is on screen and the shortcuts are on, the site also blocks the browser shortcuts that would pop up a dialog or leave the page in the middle of a run: Ctrl+S, Ctrl+P, Ctrl+D, Ctrl+H, Ctrl+J and Alt+←/→. A website cannot block tab or window shortcuts, or keys that an extension or screen reader grabs first.

          Turn the site's shortcuts off and none of this happens: your own shortcuts work normally everywhere. The mouse, touch and Esc always work.
